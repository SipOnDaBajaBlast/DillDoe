Privacy & advertising

Updated October 10, 2026.

Advertising

This website includes Google AdSense code. When advertising is available, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Advertising cookies can help show ads based on previous visits to this or other websites.

You can manage Google personalized advertising in My Ad Center. You can also learn about participating vendors and opt out of their personalized advertising at YourAdChoices. Browser settings let you manage or delete cookies.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Advertising areas are labeled “Advertisement.” Ad availability depends on Google’s approval and ad supply.

Games and browser storage

Games may store downloaded files, progress, saves, or preferences in your browser. Clearing this website’s stored data can remove those files and saves. Online games may connect to multiplayer services.

External services

Pages may load games, images, fonts, and other resources from external providers. Those providers receive the network information needed to handle requests, such as IP addresses and browser information. Embedded games and linked websites may have their own privacy policies and storage practices.

Hosting

This website uses Cloudflare hosting, with its former Vercel address used to redirect visitors. Hosting providers process network requests to deliver pages and protect their services. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Vercel’s privacy policy.
