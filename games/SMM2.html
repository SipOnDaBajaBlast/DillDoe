← Game Library Super Mario Maker 2

Web port of Super Mario Maker 2 (SMM2 Recomp). Needs Chrome or Edge 133+ for 64-bit WebAssembly memory and WebGPU, and about 3.1 GB of free disk: the first start copies the whole game into the browser's private storage, so keep the tab open until the PLAY button appears. Story mode, the editor and local play work; online levels and course upload don't, because they need a socket proxy a static site can't run. Mouse and keyboard or a gamepad.
