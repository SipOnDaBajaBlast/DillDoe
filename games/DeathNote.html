Death Note

Play the Unity browser edition with a desktop keyboard and mouse. The first launch downloads about 210 MB of game files.

Controls

Mouse
Click menus and room objects. Click the desk to move closer, then the monitor to use the PC, or the book to open the Death Note. Press and drag to flip its pages.
Keyboard
Type names or terminal commands when a text field is active.
Escape
Step back to the room view, then open or close the pause menu.

After loading, choose “Click to enter” to enable sound. Use the pause menu’s save options before leaving. Saves stay in this browser; clearing the site’s browser data deletes them.
