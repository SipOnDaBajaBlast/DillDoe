Cooking Mama

Chop, stir, and cook through Mama’s recipes in the original Nintendo DS game. The emulator runs in this tab and keeps your save in this browser.

33.5 MB ROM Stylus or mouse Keyboard & gamepad

Ready when you are.

Controls and saves

Stylus: click and drag on the lower screen to touch. The upper screen is display only.

Keyboard: arrow keys are the D-pad, Enter is Start, Shift is Select, X is A, Z is B, S is X, A is Y, Q/W are L/R, and Esc opens the emulator menu.

Gamepads are picked up automatically once connected.

Progress is auto-saved into this browser’s storage when the game writes a save. Clearing site data erases it. The first load downloads the 7.5 MB archive and unpacks the 33.5 MB ROM in the page.

Emulator: Desmond, an embeddable DeSmuME build. Emulation runs entirely on this device.

Drag on the lower screen to use the stylus. Press Enter for Start.
