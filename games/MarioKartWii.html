Mario Kart Wii is not ready to play yet

This browser port needs a Mario Kart Wii PAL disc image (RMCP01) to build its game files. The Wii Sports and Wind Waker disc images in Downloads cannot be used for it.

When the correct disc is available, the site can build and host the browser version. The game itself needs a recent Chrome or Edge browser with WebGPU and WebAssembly JSPI.
