Mario Kart Wii is not ready to play yet

The USA disc (RMCE01) is available and was recognized by a browser emulator, but it stopped at the Wii safety screen. That emulator build does not support Wii Remote input.

The WebCompiled port can build a browser game from the PAL disc (RMCP01), which is a different version. Its browser build also requires recent Chrome or Edge with WebGPU and WebAssembly JSPI.
