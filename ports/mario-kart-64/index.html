Notes

Mouse & touch: an on-screen pad sits over the game — drag the stick to steer, click the buttons for A/B/Z/R/L/Start. Toggle it with the Pad button (top right) to see the full picture.

Gamepad: plug in an Xbox or PlayStation controller and it maps itself — analog stick steers, A accelerates.

Save states & SRAM live in your browser storage, so a cup you started today is still there tomorrow.
